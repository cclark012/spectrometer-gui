## Target acceptance

1. Start the candidate using the existing emulator mode on the lab Python 3.14/PySide6 environment. Run python -m pytest -q, including the new Qt tests. Check full MainWindow startup and shutdown before connecting real instruments.

2. Use different known powers on channels 1/2. Switch each monitor and spectrum association among channel 1, channel 2 and both. Check normalization, power axes, map/3D values, statistics, toolbar labels, and exported display columns. Verify an unassociated channel gives no fabricated spectrum metric.

3. Use Reset timer before and during data collection. Confirm retained points become negative, new points advance from zero, Clear does not reset the origin, and full-run power logs retain their original timeline.

4. Capture a background, export it, and overlay it. Acquire with background subtraction and smoothing; confirm both traces and all three CSV stages. Reopen that CSV. Change exposure and confirm strict mismatch handling rather than silent scaling.

5. Apply horizontal/vertical speed changes on the iDus. Confirm the timing labels update without closing Details and agree with capability readback. Test filename and Autosave changes from both toolbar and settings.

6. Run a short exposure sweep and a time series at 0, 10, 20 s. Then deliberately use an acquisition longer than the schedule interval: verify one file per point, no overlap, positive recorded lateness and a matching summary UUID/count.

7. Run a small reversible Kymera center sweep. Abort once during motion and once during exposure; verify ownership remains locked until completion and optional center restoration. Confirm saved grids/calibration/state match each applied center. Check close during a running scan.

8. Connect the actual Lake Shore 335 COM port, compare A/B and units with its front panel, then test disconnect/reconnect and invalid-sensor status. Confirm no setpoints, heater ranges or outputs change. Test temperature trigger tolerance, dwell, timeout, stale readings and CCD temperature queries.

9. Keep the pre-existing outstanding hardware checklist. This release does not close QEPro reconnect/buffer validation, broader Andor owner regression, cooler-warning/restart issues, OBIS physical interlock truth, or detector/output-port mapping. Gated timing development is deferred at the user’s request.
